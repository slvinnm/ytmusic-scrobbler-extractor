Guide & Instructions

Part 1: Get YouTube History

  1. Go to Google Takeout (takeout.google.com).
  2. Click "Deselect all" at the top.
  3. Scroll down and check "YouTube and YouTube Music".
  4. Click "Multiple formats" and change History format to JSON. Click OK.
  5. Click "All YouTube data included", deselect all, and select ONLY history. Click OK.
  6. Scroll to the bottom, click Next step, then Create export.
  7. Wait for the email, download the ZIP, extract it, and find your watch-history.json file.

Part 2: Scrobble with Scrubbler-2

  1. Download and open Scrubbler-2 ↗.
  2. Log into your Last.fm account inside the application.
  3. Select the File Parse Scrobbler module.
  4. Change the parser format dropdown type to JSON.
  5. Import the generated formatted.json file(s) downloaded from this tool and click Parse!
  6. Verify your tracks in the parsed view and click Scrobble.
